# Credits

Third-party assets shipped with this project, and where they came from.

## Sound

Royalty-free clips from the Pixabay and Freesound community libraries, trimmed and converted to mono Ogg
Vorbis. They live under `src/main/resources/assets/tfmg/sounds/`.

| File | Original clip | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pipeline/station_motor.ogg` | freesound_community — *air condition motor noise* | `tfmg:pipeline.station_motor`, the loop a Booster Station plays while it is turning |
| `pipeline/station_fluid.ogg` | freesound_community — *basement water pump* | `tfmg:pipeline.station_fluid`, layered over the motor while the station is actually moving fluid |
| `pipeline/pipe_strain.ogg` | freesound_community — *metal poppy tranzeence snaps* | `tfmg:pipeline.pipe_strain`, the sound a pipe makes while it is carrying more pressure than it is rated for |
| `pipeline/pipe_burst.ogg` | freesound_community — *metal hit cartoon* | `tfmg:pipeline.pipe_burst`, the sound a pipe makes when it lets go |

Everything else in this repository is either original to Create: The Factory Must Grow or written for this
project, under the terms of the MIT licence. See [LICENCE.md](LICENCE.md).
